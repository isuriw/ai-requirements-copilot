# Product Vision

## Product Name

ReqPilot

## Tagline

A single source of truth for business requirements.

---

# Vision

ReqPilot is an AI-powered requirements management platform that helps organisations transform scattered business knowledge into a trusted, structured and traceable requirements repository.

Rather than replacing Business Analysts, ReqPilot assists them by extracting, organising, validating and maintaining requirements while keeping business users in control of approvals.

The goal is to provide a single source of truth that can be trusted by Business Analysts, Product Owners, Solution Designers, Developers and Testers.

---

# Problem Statement

Project requirements are often scattered across multiple locations including:

- Word documents
- Confluence pages
- Emails
- Meeting notes
- Jira
- Teams conversations

As projects progress, requirements become duplicated, inconsistent and difficult to trace.

Teams waste time asking:

"Which requirement is correct?"

ReqPilot provides one trusted location where requirements are reviewed, approved and consumed by delivery teams.

---

# Success Statement

Every requirement used by developers and testers has been reviewed and approved by the business.

Everyone works from the same version.
