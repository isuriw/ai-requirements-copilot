# Vision Statement

## Project Name

ReqPilot

## Vision

ReqPilot is an AI-powered assistant designed to help Business Analysts, Product Owners and Solution Designers analyse business documents and transform them into structured, high-quality requirements.

The platform reduces manual effort by identifying ambiguities, extracting requirements, generating user stories and suggesting clarifying questions using Large Language Models (LLMs).

Rather than replacing analysts, ReqPilot acts as an intelligent co-pilot that accelerates the requirements discovery and solution design process.

---

## Problem Statement

Business requirements are often documented across Word documents, meeting notes, emails and Confluence pages.

These documents are frequently:

- incomplete
- ambiguous
- inconsistent
- duplicated
- difficult to analyse

Analysts spend considerable time reading documentation, identifying missing information and translating business needs into structured requirements.

---

## Vision

Reduce the time required to analyse business documentation while improving requirement quality and consistency through AI-assisted analysis.

---

## Target Users

- Business Analysts
- Product Owners
- Solution Designers
- Technical Leads
- Architects

---

## Success Criteria

Users can upload a document and receive:

- Executive summary
- Functional requirements
- Ambiguous statements
- Clarification questions
- Suggested user stories
- Acceptance criteria
