# Functional Requirements

## Version

0.1

---

# Goal

ReqPilot assists Business Analysts and Solution Designers by analysing business documents using AI.

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR1

The system shall allow a user to upload a Word document.

---

## FR2

The system shall extract text from the uploaded document.

---

## FR3

The system shall generate an executive summary.

---

## FR4

The system shall identify functional requirements.

---

## FR5

The system shall identify ambiguous or unclear requirements.

---

## FR6

The system shall generate clarification questions.

---

## FR7

The system shall display all generated outputs in the web application.
